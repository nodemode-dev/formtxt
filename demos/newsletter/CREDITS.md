# Demo credits

Nothing in this demo is third-party media. "The Riverside Post: Autumn 2026" (`frontend/public/demos/riverside-newsletter.ftxt`) was made for the demo by `scripts/demo-docs.py` with the engine's own document model; the Riverside Residents' Association, the footbridge, the repair cafe, the events and every figure are invented. Its two pictures, the river meadows at dusk and the second one inside the columns, were drawn by the same script with Pillow; they are not photographs. The type is Playfair Display, Lora and Work Sans, bundled with the app and listed in `NOTICE.md`.

The page loads its fonts, covers and icon data from `demo/assets`; `demo/CREDITS.md` lists them and the third-party works inside the script.

The app opens the same demo from Home > Demos, Help > Demos and the `demo.newsletter` command, from the same file, so this note covers it too.
