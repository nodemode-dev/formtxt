# Demo credits

Nothing in this demo is third-party media. "How fast mud settles on the north shore flats" (`frontend/public/demos/tidal-flats-paper.ftxt`) was made for the demo by `scripts/demo-docs.py` with the engine's own document model; the Saltmarsh Field Station, Rosa Hale, Daniel Mbeki, the measurements and every cited work are invented. The type is Libre Baskerville and Source Serif 4, and the equations use the KaTeX fonts, all bundled with the app and listed in `NOTICE.md`.

The page loads its fonts, covers and icon data from `demo/assets`; `demo/CREDITS.md` lists them and the third-party works inside the script.

The app opens the same demo from Home > Demos, Help > Demos and the `demo.paper` command, from the same file, so this note covers it too.
